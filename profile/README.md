## 🎯 What is Moovv?

**Moovv Fit** is your AI-powered personal mobility coach. We help you relieve joint pain, fix posture, and move freely through personalized exercise routines designed by certified physiotherapists.

### Key Features
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Plans** — Personalized routines that adapt to your progress
- 👨‍⚕️ **Physio-Verified** — Every exercise vetted by certified MSK specialists
- ⏱️ **Daily Micro-Workouts** — 10-15 minute sessions that fit your schedule
- 📊 **Progress Tracking** — Monitor improvements in flexibility and pain levels

## 👥 Who Uses Moovv?

- 💼 Desk workers with back and neck stiffness
- 🎾 Athletes improving mobility and preventing injury
- 🦴 Anyone recovering from joint pain
